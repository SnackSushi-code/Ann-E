# CAD and Additive Manufacturing

**Tools and processes:** Shapr3D, CAD modeling, FDM 3D printing  
**Status:** Ongoing personal design and prototyping work.

## Overview

Developing practical CAD and additive-manufacturing skills by moving from a design concept to a printable physical prototype.

## Representative work

- Creating and refining 3D CAD geometry in Shapr3D.
- Considering fit, button access, clearances, orientation, and printability during design iteration.
- Preparing designs for FDM 3D printing and evaluating physical fit.
- Iterating on a custom automotive key-cover concept for a 2026 Volkswagen Jetta.

## Skills demonstrated

3D CAD modeling, spatial reasoning, design iteration, additive-manufacturing fundamentals, prototype evaluation, and attention to functional constraints.

## Portfolio materials

Original CAD exports, screenshots/renders, slicer settings, and photographs of printed prototypes will be added as the design progresses. The project is identified as ongoing rather than a completed production-ready design.
